# Writing Skill

## When to Use

- Creating articles, blog posts, or documentation
- Writing marketing copy, emails, or communications
- Editing or improving existing content
- Generating creative content (stories, scripts, poems)
- Translating or adapting content for different audiences
- Summarizing or restructuring complex information

## Instructions

### Writing Process

1. **Understand the audience**: Who will read this? What's their level?
2. **Define the purpose**: Inform, persuade, entertain, or instruct?
3. **Choose the tone**: Formal, conversational, technical, or creative?
4. **Structure the content**: Clear introduction, logical flow, strong conclusion
5. **Draft and refine**: Write first, then improve iteratively

### Content Types & Guidelines

#### Technical Documentation
- Use clear, precise language
- Include examples and code snippets where helpful
- Structure with headings, lists, and tables
- Define technical terms on first use
- Follow consistent formatting

#### Marketing & Persuasive Content
- Lead with benefits, not features
- Use active voice and strong verbs
- Include social proof or data points
- End with clear call-to-action
- Keep paragraphs short (2-3 sentences)

#### Articles & Blog Posts
- Compelling headline and introduction
- Use subheadings for scannability
- Support claims with evidence
- Include practical examples or case studies
- Conclude with actionable takeaways

#### Creative Writing
- Establish voice and perspective early
- Show, don't tell
- Use sensory details and specific imagery
- Maintain consistent tone and pacing
- End with impact

### Output Quality Checklist

- [ ] Clear purpose and audience alignment
- [ ] Logical structure and flow
- [ ] Appropriate tone and language level
- [ ] No grammatical errors or typos
- [ ] Actionable or memorable conclusion
- [ ] Proper formatting and readability
